Circular materials feasibility

Eligibility conditions

Applicants must be established in the Netherlands.

The proposed project must investigate the technical or commercial feasibility of a circular materials process.

The project must take place in the sample programme’s eligible region.

Example funding

The example grant covers 40% of reviewed eligible project costs, up to €40,000. These figures are illustrative.

Application questions

Describe the project, the problem it addresses and the result you want to achieve. Explain what will be tested during the feasibility study.

Explain which technical questions remain open, how you will test them and what evidence will determine whether the process is feasible.

Describe the intended environmental and commercial benefits. Include measurable outcomes where available.

Maximum 200 words.
